These are multi-stage 2 GHz RF amplifier circuits (discrete BJT cascaded design and a hybrid BJT + op-amp variant) for signal amplification, impedance matching, and linearity analysis in high-frequency applications.

Could be used as 300 MHz RADAR or Motion Proximity Sensor (up to ~10m). Antenna output is connected to the 100pF capacitor on the collector; here a transmission line is used.

Objects moving towards the antenna generate positive voltages, and objects moving away generate negative voltages. Thus moving an object back and forth will cause the LED to blink on and off.

This circuit is almost identical to the VT Fuze circuit from WW2, except has been modernised with transistors instead of tubes.
